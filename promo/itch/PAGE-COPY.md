## Page description

### A whole mix, opened up.

PRISM is a free, browser-based audio studio for separating a stereo song into stems and working with them on a multitrack timeline.

**[OPEN THE FREE STUDIO →](https://vicsanity623.github.io/)**

### Separate, edit, export

- Split a stereo mix into **vocals, drums, bass and other** with HTDemucs AI running in your browser.
- Use the multitrack timeline to play, arrange, trim and split clips.
- Shape tracks with built-in effects, frequency tools, a spectrogram and analyzer.
- Export a mix or stems as WAV/MP3, or save a project bundle.
- Try DSP separation modes without downloading the AI model.

### Runs on your device

Your selected audio is processed in the browser; PRISM does not upload it to a PRISM audio server. The first AI run downloads the HTDemucs-FT model (about **166 MB**) from Hugging Face. The browser caches it for later use when possible. A recent desktop browser and several GB of available memory are recommended.

### Before you start

- AI separation is resource-intensive and can be slow on some devices.
- Separation quality varies by song; bleed and artifacts are normal.
- **MDX-Net is not available in the hosted browser version.**
- No account is required to use the editor. Google sign-in is optional.
- Use only audio you own or have permission to process and publish.

PRISM is free to use and supported by advertising on its website. Ads are not part of the audio-processing workflow. See the [Privacy Policy](https://vicsanity623.github.io/privacy.html) and [How-to guide](https://vicsanity623.github.io/how-to.html).
