Privacy
policy.

This policy explains what happens when you visit the PRISM website and use its browser-based audio editor.

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 21, 2026

Audio and project files

Audio files selected in the PRISM editor are processed locally in your browser. The editor is designed not to upload those files to a PRISM-operated server. Projects and exports are stored or downloaded by your browser, not synced to a PRISM account.

If you choose AI separation, the browser downloads model files from Hugging Face. That request may disclose ordinary connection information such as your IP address and browser request details to the model host. Model files may be cached in your browser storage.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising on informational pages. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, limit fraud and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Google may process information such as device identifiers, browser information, IP address and interactions with ads. Your choices and applicable law may affect whether ads are personalized.

Learn more about Google's advertising technologies and manage ad personalization in My Ad Center. You can also adjust cookie settings in your browser. Blocking cookies may affect advertising and some site functions.

Optional Google sign-in

Google sign-in is optional and is not required to use the editor. If you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication and Google process account information such as your Google account identifier, display name, email address and profile image according to their privacy policies. In this version, signing in does not upload audio or create cloud-saved projects.

Hosting and third-party services

The site is served as static files by its hosting provider. The host may receive standard web request logs, including IP address, requested URL, browser user agent, referrer and timestamp, for delivery, security and diagnostics. The AI model host and advertising provider have their own privacy practices. This site does not operate an audio upload API.

Children and sensitive information

The site is a general-purpose audio tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not include personal or confidential information in filenames or other data you choose to share with third-party services.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the site or its services change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the site owner through the repository issue page; do not post private information publicly.

This page is a plain-language site disclosure, not legal advice. The site owner should review it against their actual AdSense settings, hosting configuration and applicable privacy laws before publication.
