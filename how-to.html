FIELD GUIDE / GETTING STARTED

Make your first
stem session.

PRISM runs in a modern browser. Load a track, choose a separation method, then edit the resulting stems in the built-in multitrack workspace.

ABOUT 5 MINUTES · NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED

1. Open the studio and load audio

Go to the PRISM studio in a recent desktop version of Chrome, Edge or Safari. Drag an audio file into the Source panel or use Browse. Audio is decoded locally in your browser. Use audio you own or have permission to process.

Supported formats depend on the browser's audio decoder; WAV and MP3 are the safest choices. Very long or high-resolution files can use substantial memory, especially on phones. To prevent mobile tabs from being killed during AI inference, HTDemucs AI is disabled on phones and tablets; Fast, Studio and Deep DSP modes remain available there. Use a desktop browser for AI separation.

2. Choose how to separate

For AI separation, choose AI and use HTDemucs-FT. The first run fetches an approximately 166 MB ONNX model from Hugging Face. It is cached on your device for later sessions. AI separation uses significant memory and may take longer than the track itself on some computers.

The app supports HTDemucs models in the browser. MDX-Net is not currently available in this hosted browser build; the MDX option in the desktop source relies on a separately run local server.

For lighter processing without a model download, try the Fast, Studio or Deep DSP modes. These use signal-processing techniques rather than the AI model, and their results differ from neural source separation.

3. Edit your stems

After separation, the stems appear as tracks in the timeline. Use the transport controls to play and stop, adjust track levels in the right panel, and use the editing tools to split, trim, move or duplicate clips. The Spectrogram and Analyzer tabs help inspect the sound. Effects and Frequency Lab controls are available for additional shaping.

4. Export or save your session

Use Export to render a mix, an individual track, all stems or a project bundle. Formats include WAV and MP3 where supported. Keep your exported files somewhere safe: the editor works locally and does not sync projects to an account or cloud drive.

Privacy and performance notes

  • Your selected audio is processed on your device by the editor; it is not uploaded to PRISM.
  • The AI model is downloaded from Hugging Face only when you choose AI separation.
  • Allow enough free memory; close other demanding tabs if the browser struggles.
  • AI results are estimates, not isolated studio recordings. Some bleed and artifacts are normal.
